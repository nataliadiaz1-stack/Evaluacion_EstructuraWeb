About Us

Florasis is an innovative makeup brand founded in 2017, known for combining ancient skincare wisdom with modern makeup artistry. The brand focuses on using nourishing flower essences in its products, which are designed to promote both makeup and skincare benefits. Florasis emphasizes Chinese aesthetics in its visually stunning packaging and product design.
